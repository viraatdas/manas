## Sync that keeps what you did, and shared groups that say whose they are

- Checking something off on your iPhone no longer gets undone by the Mac's next check-in, and a todo you delete on one device stays deleted on the other. When both devices change the same todo, each keeps its own change instead of one overwriting the other.
- A change made on one device a moment before the other synced could be missed for good. It can't be any more.
- One change the server refused used to stop everything after it from syncing, silently. Now the rest still syncs, and the sync line says how many changes were refused.
- A shared group whose name matches one of your own groups no longer turns into two identical "Manas" buckets. The shared one says whose it is, the picker tells them apart, and the share sheet offers to fold your private one into it.
- Sharing your own group while you're in someone else's group of the same name works again, and the share sheet says exactly why an invite didn't go through.
- The compose bar no longer opens pre-set to a group you filed something into days ago.
