## Checking something off makes a sound

- Ticking a todo now gives a short pop, on both the Mac and the iPhone. Unchecking stays silent — the pop is the sound of finishing something, and hearing it while undoing reads as the app misunderstanding you.
- It's the same sound on both, generated rather than borrowed, so the two feel like one app rather than whatever noise each platform happened to have lying around.
- It stays out of the way: your music keeps playing, the ring switch silences it on iPhone, and on Mac it follows the "Play user interface sound effects" setting in System Settings › Sound.
