## People in a shared list have names now

- A shared group showed a person's phone number, with their initials in the circle right beside it and a "Name" button offering to fix a name your phone already knew. The number and the initials were being worked out separately, and only one of them ever asked your address book.
- Everyone in a shared group now reads as their name, taken from your own contacts. Their number moves underneath it rather than being replaced by it, so you can still see who a row belongs to at a glance.
- Nobody has to be named by hand any more. The "Name" button only appears for someone your address book genuinely doesn't know — which is the one case where typing a name actually tells Manas something new.
- Names are still read on your device, drawn, and forgotten. Nothing is uploaded, so the same group reads "Krithik Rao" on a phone that has him in its contacts and stays as digits on one that doesn't.
