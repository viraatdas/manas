## Crossing something off keeps it off

- A todo you finished could come back, uncrossed, at the top of the next day's list. Manas rolls yesterday's unfinished work onto today when it opens — but a device that had been closed since yesterday was doing that from its own stale picture, before hearing from the server. So a todo you crossed off on the Mac got rolled forward by the phone the next morning, and because moving it counts as an edit, the phone's stale copy won and the finished todo came back on both.
- Rolling the day forward now waits for the first sync to land. If you're offline, it still rolls over as before — arriving a moment late is a nuisance, losing the day isn't acceptable.
