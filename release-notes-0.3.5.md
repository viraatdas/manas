## Folding a group stays where you put it

- Collapsing or expanding a group no longer throws the scroll. Clicking "Work" was folding Work on *every* day in the feed at once, including the days above what you were looking at — the list above shrank, and the page jumped out from under the cursor. Groups now fold per day: the header you click holds still, and only the rows below it move.
- Each day remembers its own folded groups across launches.

## Opening on today

- Manas opens on today again. It could land days back in your history instead, because it scrolled toward today before the feed had measured itself. It now checks where it actually arrived and corrects.
- Jumping to today from deep in the timeline had the same problem and the same fix.

## One way to get to today

- The floating Today pill answered to ⌘T while the menu and the header button used ⌘L, and it skipped the cursor hand-off the other two did. All three now do the same thing: bring today to the top and put the cursor in the compose bar.

## Smaller things

- Folding a group or the suggestions card honors Reduce Motion.
