## When names can't load, Manas says so

- If Manas was never allowed to read your Contacts, everyone in a shared group showed up as a phone number and nothing said why — which looks exactly like nobody in the group being in your address book. Only one of those is fixable, so the share panel now tells you which one it is and takes you straight to the setting.
- Manas also gave up too easily. If the permission prompt never actually appeared — because the app was hidden, or had no window to show it from — it counted that as having asked and never tried again, so names stayed off with no way back short of restarting. It now asks again the next time there's somewhere to show the prompt.
