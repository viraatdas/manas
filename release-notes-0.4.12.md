## Your list is current the moment you look at it

- Manas no longer shows yesterday's list after a restart or a night asleep. It now pulls from the cloud on launch, on wake, and whenever you come back to the window — while still painting what it already has instantly, so the first frame is never blank.
- Today stops listing groups that hold nothing. A day with one todo in Work used to show Work plus five or six empty rows reading 0/0 over a "Drag todos here" strip — Personal, Waste of time, every custom group, every shared group. Those buckets exist to be drop targets, so they now appear only while you are actually dragging a todo, and stay out of the way the rest of the time.
- The app icon is back to the coral mark on an off-white tile, matching the iPhone app again.
- On iPhone: tap a todo's text to edit it in place, instead of holding it and picking Edit from a menu. Tap the Manas wordmark to jump the feed back to today.
