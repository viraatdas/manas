## Every browser, and a time count that tells the truth

- Manas now reads history from every browser on your Mac — Arc, Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Dia and Safari — instead of Arc alone. The source is called Browser rather than Arc, and a site you had open in two browsers counts once rather than twice.
- The Waste of time total has stopped exaggerating. Manas re-reads the whole day every hour and the judge renames what it finds each time, so one afternoon on X arrived as "X scrolling", then "X/Twitter scrolling", then "Scrolled X and Instagram" — eleven wordings of the same session, adding up to 420 minutes of a day that held about 60. Wordings of the same time sink now count once, at the largest estimate of it; genuinely separate ones still add up.
- That total is written the way you would say it — "~1h" rather than "420 min". It is inferred from browsing history, so it was never accurate to the minute and no longer pretends to be.
- Today no longer lists groups holding nothing. A day with one todo in Work used to show it alongside five or six empty rows reading 0/0; those buckets now appear only while you are dragging a todo into one.
- The version number jumps to 1.0.0 to line up with the iPhone app, which is already on 1.0. From here both platforms share a major and minor version so it is obvious which builds belong together.
