## Todos file themselves

- Manas now sorts your list into project and theme groups on its own. Add "car wash miata" and it lands under Car alongside the service reminder, instead of sitting loose at the bottom.
- It reuses the groups you already have rather than coining a synonym, so a theme doesn't split into two piles that mean the same thing.
- Anything you filed yourself stays where you put it. Auto-grouping only fills in todos that have no group — a check-in will never reshuffle what you sorted by hand.
