## Sharing a group is one step now

- Pick the person and it's shared. No number to type, no name to fill in, no second button to press — their name comes across from your contact card so their avatar looks like them.
- The "you appear as" field is out of the way. It's about you rather than about the group, so it now sits once in Settings next to your phone sign-in, instead of in every share panel.
- Typing a number still works for anyone who isn't in your contacts.
