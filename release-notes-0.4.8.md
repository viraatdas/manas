## The people in a shared group read properly now

- Someone nobody had named showed their phone number twice — once as their name, and once again underneath it. The number now appears once, on the line where it belongs.
- It was impossible to tell whether a name was shared or not. Names pulled from your own address book are marked, and they stay on your device: the same person can read as "Krithik Rao" to you and as a phone number to everyone else in the group, because that name is yours, not the group's.
- The panel now says which is which in plain words, so naming someone tells you what it actually did.

## Your own name works the way you'd expect

- Setting your name from a group's share panel only applied to that one group, and left the name field in Settings empty — so it looked like nothing had saved. Your name is now one name: set it anywhere and it's how you appear in every group you're in.
- Your row has a "Your name" button where the name goes, instead of hiding it in Settings.
