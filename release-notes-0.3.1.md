## Fold away what you're not working on

- Tap a group header to collapse it, on both the desktop and the iPhone. The done/total tally stays visible while it's folded, so a collapsed group still tells you what's left in it.
- "You might have also done this" folds too.
- Collapsed state is remembered per group and stays local to each device — folding a group on the desktop doesn't fold it on your phone.
- On the desktop a folded group is still a drop target: drag a todo onto a collapsed header and it files there.

## Anonymous usage reporting actually works now

- Manas has always had a "Share anonymous usage" switch, but no build ever shipped with the reporting key attached, so it never had anywhere to send. It does now — on both platforms.
- It stays off until you say yes. The app asks once on launch and takes no for an answer; the switch lives in the gear popover either way.
- What it reports is counts and timings — app opens, check-ins, groups created. Never your todos, messages, browsing, or anything the judge reads.
