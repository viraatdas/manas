## Light, always

- Manas is a light-only app now. It no longer follows your system appearance on either the desktop or the iPhone.

## Knowing where you are

- The top-left says which day you're looking at and follows the feed as you scroll — "Today" while you're on today, "Monday, Jul 27" once you've scrolled back into the week.
- A new button in the header returns you to today from anywhere in the timeline (⌘L still does it too).

## Commitments land as work, not as history

- Things Manas picks up from your conversations — "I'll send the invite", "your order is ready for pickup" — now arrive as open todos when you add them. They were being filed already checked off, which ticked off the very thing the reminder existed for.
- Suggestions are split accordingly: what you said you'd do sits above what you might have also done.
