## Share a group with someone

- Any todo group can now be shared with a phone number. The person you share it with sees the same bucket in their own Manas, adds to it, and ticks things off — on the Mac and on the iPhone.
- Every line in a shared group carries a small circular avatar on the right for whoever wrote it: their initials if they set a name, otherwise the last two digits of their number, in a color that stays the same for them everywhere.
- The group header wears the people it is shared with, and opens the share panel: invite by number, see who is in, remove someone, stop sharing, or leave a group you were invited to.
- A shared group and a private group with the same name stay two separate buckets. Neither can pour into the other, and the check-in only ever labels a todo — it never files one into someone else's share.

## Your rows stay yours

- A todo in a shared group can be read and ticked off by the people you shared it with — that is the point — but it cannot be carried out of that group by anyone except you. Nobody you share one list with can move your line into a different list of theirs, in front of people you never shared it with.
- Nor can they sign your row over to themselves, or make the avatar name the wrong person. The server enforces all three, not just the app.

## Smaller things

- The iPhone compose bar and the move-to menu both offer shared buckets alongside private groups.
- Sharing a group with someone exposes that group and nothing else — the rest of your todos stay as private as they were.
